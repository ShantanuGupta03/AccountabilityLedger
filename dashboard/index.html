Who held
the file?

A portfolio view of the cases in this ledger. It groups cases by who held the relevant office at the time. The figures are the totals recorded in those cases, and they are not attributed to the office-holder as an individual.

Read every figure below as "recorded in cases under this portfolio", never as "caused by this person". Holding an office at the time a failure happened is a matter of public record. It is not a finding of personal responsibility for any death or any rupee, and nothing on this page should be quoted as one.

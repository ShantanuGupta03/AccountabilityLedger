+ How to read this ledger. Method, figures and limits

This is an opinion and analysis resource that makes the accountability case. Holding the ruling party to account is the right of every citizen of a democracy, and this document exercises that right without apology. Read it as an indictment, not as a neutral encyclopedia.

Its force is its accuracy, so fact and allegation are kept strictly apart. Court verdicts, official audits, government admissions and documented death tolls are stated as fact. Where a scam or personal wrongdoing is alleged, it sits in a marked "Contested / alleged" field, attributed to the opposition, to reporting, or to a court process, and never asserted here as proven. Some matters remain in court. Each case also records the government's own defence, because an indictment that survives the counter-argument is the one that cannot be waved away.

"Ministers responsible" names who held the portfolio. That is a matter of record, not by itself a charge of personal guilt. Several failures belong to state BJP governments and are labelled as such rather than pinned on the Union.

Every number tagged EST is an estimate, often a disputed range. Treat it as order of magnitude and verify before quoting.

The headline cost and human-toll figures are broad, cumulative estimates. They sum only the explicit per-case estimates recorded in the data file, including disputed and range-based figures; cases can overlap and the totals are not an audit or a claim that every amount was stolen.

Sources: most cases carry verified links; a few are marked "add link" for you to fill and check. Nothing is invented. This is a large catalogue, not a complete one. The data lives in one local JSON file, built for you to keep adding.

What this is

A citizen's instrument of oversight in the world's largest democracy. It measures the government against one test that power hates: was anyone ever made to answer? It is deliberately critical, and deliberately open about being so, so that readers judge for themselves.

Extend it. Every case is one block in the DATA list at the top of the script. Copy a block, name the ministers, add verified links. The largest democracy on earth deserves a permanent, open accountability ledger. This is a beginning, not the whole of it.

Before you publish

Replace every "add link" with a checked source. Treat all EST figures as approximate. Keep allegations attributed. Confirm the current legal status of anything still in court, since several cases here are still moving.

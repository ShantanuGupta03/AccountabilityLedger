Every submission is private, screened for evidence and legal risk, then reviewed by an editor. Submission does not guarantee publication.

Before submitting

  • Describe a specific incident, policy failure, or public-accountability concern.
  • Provide at least one credible public source. Do not submit private personal data.
  • Separate established facts from allegations and identify the source of each allegation.
